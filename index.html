I recently completed my Ph.D. at McGill University under the supervision of Adrian Vetta. I am now starting a postdoctoral position with the RIKEN AIP Computational Social Choice Team directed by Ayumi Igarashi. My research interests are in Algorithmic Game Theory, Fair Division, Graph Theory, and Approximation Algorithms.
