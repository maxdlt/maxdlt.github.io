About

I completed my Ph.D. at McGill University under the supervision of Adrian Vetta. I am now a postdoctoral researcher with the RIKEN AIP Computational Social Choice Team, directed by Ayumi Igarashi.

My research interests include algorithmic game theory, fair division, graph theory, and approximation algorithms.
